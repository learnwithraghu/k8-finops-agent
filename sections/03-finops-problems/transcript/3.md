# Video: Finding Labels in Two Namespaces

**Time Budget:** 3–4 min

### Opening

When you are doing FinOps work in the cluster, you do not want one giant command that dumps everything at once. You want to inspect the resources the same way you would in real life: a namespace at a time, a workload type at a time.

Today we are just looking for labels — and more importantly, how those labels change from one namespace to another.

---

### Inventory namespace

Start in `inventory`.

```bash
kubectl get deployments -n inventory --show-labels
kubectl get services -n inventory --show-labels
```

Look at the label keys and values. You are checking whether the team followed the same naming pattern across its workloads.

---

### Payment namespace

Now switch to `payment`.

```bash
kubectl get deployments -n payment --show-labels
kubectl get services -n payment --show-labels
```

This is the point of the demo: the labels are not always the same, even when the resource type is the same. One namespace might use one style, another namespace might use something slightly different.

---

### Close

If you have to hunt for labels namespace by namespace, consistency is already a problem. That is the pain we want to surface here — before we ever get to automation.
